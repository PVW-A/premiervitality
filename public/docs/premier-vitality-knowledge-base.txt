SECTION 8: VITALITY SCORE
================================================================================

What It Is:
The Vitality Score is a comprehensive health metric — a single number from 0 to 100 that aggregates biomarkers across hormones, metabolic health, lipids, thyroid, inflammation, liver & kidney function, and more. It gives you an instant snapshot of your overall wellness and shows exactly which areas need attention.

How It's Calculated:
Each biomarker is graded against OPTIMAL ranges (not just normal reference ranges) and assigned a score. Markers in the optimal zone score 100, normal scores 70, and out-of-range markers score lower depending on severity. Your overall Vitality Score is the weighted average across all tested markers.

Free Access:
Anyone can create a free account, upload existing bloodwork (a photo or PDF), and receive their Vitality Score with a full category breakdown and personalized improvement tips — no appointment or membership required. The AI reads the bloodwork automatically and generates the score within seconds.

Actionable Insights:
Every marker outside the optimal range comes with specific, actionable tips — from diet and supplement recommendations to lifestyle changes. Users can see their top areas to improve at a glance and drill into each category for detailed guidance.

Categories Tracked:
- Hormonal Health
- Metabolic Health
- Cardiovascular/Lipid Health
- Thyroid Function
- Inflammatory Markers
- Liver Function
- Kidney Function
- Blood Cell Health (CBC)
